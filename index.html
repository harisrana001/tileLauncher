Zero Compromise

100% privacy transparency

TileLaunch does not collect, transmit, or monetize your data. Here is exactly why each optional Android permission is requested.

🔔

notification access

Used strictly to compute numeric unread count badges on app tiles. TileLaunch never reads, stores, or transmits the text content of your notifications.

📍

approximate location

Required only for the Weather Live Tile to fetch temperature data from Open-Meteo. Your coordinates are never uploaded to our servers or shared with advertisers.

📅

calendar read

Enables the Calendar live tile to display your next scheduled appointment. Event queries run strictly on-device using Android's local CalendarContract.

🖼️

photos & wallpaper

Allows you to set a custom home screen wallpaper photo and display photo slideshows in the Photos live tile. Zero cloud syncing or remote image uploads.

🛡️

transparent advertising

Free version includes exactly one non-intrusive interstitial upon completing onboarding. Zero banner or screen clutter. Pro unlocks a 100% ad-free experience.
