## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Experience the authentic elegance, speed, and typography of a pure Metro-style tile launcher. Designed from scratch for performance, TileLaunch brings an ultra-responsive, dynamic grid to your home screen with fluid 3D turnstile animations, live flipping tiles, and deep customization.

### Why Choose TileLaunch?

⚡ **Fluid 60 FPS Performance**
Built natively with Jetpack Compose and optimized for modern and entry-level devices alike (2 GB+ RAM). Enjoy instant app launches, responsive swipe navigation, and zero jank.

🔋 **Zero Idle Battery Drain**
Smart lifecycle-aware architecture pauses all live tile flips and internal timers the exact millisecond your screen turns off or another application is active. Enjoy dynamic widgets without sacrificing your battery life.

🔲 **The Authentic Metro Tile Grid**
Arrange your home screen exactly how you want. Choose between Small (1x1), Medium (2x2), and Wide (4x2) tiles with a pixel-perfect 6dp gap system. Tap with realistic 3D perspective tilt feedback, drag to reflow neighbor tiles dynamically, and cycle sizes with effortless animations.

🔄 **Signature Turnstile Transitions**
Watch your tiles rotate gracefully around their left axis with distance-based staggered timing whenever you launch an app, accompanied by the smooth horizontal page swoosh as you pivot between Start and the All Apps drawer.

🔠 **Alphabetical App Drawer & Quick Jump**
Swipe effortlessly to access a clean, alphabetical list of all installed apps with sticky section headers, an instant letter jump grid, live search filtering, and context menus for quick pinning, uninstalling, or viewing app info.

🎨 **Deep Personalization**
- Accent colors: Express yourself with a curated palette of vibrant, high-contrast flat colors.
- OLED Dark & Crisp Light Modes: Deep blacks that save power on AMOLED displays or high-key light mode.
- Wallpaper & Parallax: Set your favorite background photo with adjustable tile transparency and realistic 1/3-speed parallax scrolling.
- Era Packs: Switch seamlessly between the Modern 4-column grid and the Classic 2-column layout.
- Original Haptic & Sound Effects: Tactile clicks and soft sound feedback for every press and transition.

🔒 **100% Privacy Focused**
TileLaunch believes your device belongs to you. Zero tracking, zero third-party analytics, and zero remote telemetry. Notification counters and calendar events are calculated purely on-device. No ads ever appear on your home screen or app drawer, and Pro unlocks a 100% ad-free experience.

### Permissions & Privacy Transparency
TileLaunch requests only the permissions strictly required to provide its home screen and tile features:
- **Query Packages / LauncherApps**: Discover and launch your installed applications.
- **Notification Access**: Compute local numeric notification badges on app tiles (message contents are never read, stored, or transmitted).
- **Approximate Location**: Retrieve local weather data from Open-Meteo for the Weather live tile.
- **Calendar**: Display the next upcoming event on the Calendar live tile.
- **Photos / Media**: Choose custom wallpaper backgrounds and display slideshows on the Photos live tile.
