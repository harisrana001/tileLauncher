3. User Responsibilities, Permissions & Acceptable Use

3.1 Role as Android Home Screen Replacement

TileLaunch operates as an Android Home Screen (Launcher) replacement. You acknowledge and understand that configuring TileLaunch as your default launcher modifies how your device presents installed applications, shortcuts, widgets, and navigation gestures. You maintain full control over your device and may revert to your device manufacturer's default launcher at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Home App.

3.2 Permissions & Local On-Device Processing

TileLaunch utilizes the following Android permissions strictly to deliver its core launcher functionality. In accordance with our Privacy Policy, all processing occurs locally on your device:

  • Query Installed Packages (LauncherApps): Required to scan, list, and launch installed applications on your device in the Start screen grid and the alphabetical app drawer. This data remains in volatile device memory and is never transmitted off your device.
  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): Used exclusively to calculate numeric unread badge counts on relevant app tiles (e.g., missed calls, unread messages). TileLaunch never reads, stores, or transmits notification message contents or sender details.
  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Optional for the Weather Live Tile. When enabled, coarse coordinates are sent directly to the open-source, keyless Open-Meteo weather endpoint via encrypted HTTPS. Your location is never logged, stored in any database, or used for advertising profiling.
  • Calendar Read Access (READ_CALENDAR): Optional for the Calendar Live Tile to display upcoming schedule appointments. Calendar events are processed purely on-device via Android's local CalendarContract.
  • Photos & Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Optional for custom photo wallpaper backdrops and the Photos Live Tile slideshow. Images remain exclusively on your device.

3.3 No User Accounts or Server Storage

TileLaunch does not require, host, or support user accounts, passwords, or cloud database storage. All tile configurations, sizes, accent color choices, and custom layouts are saved locally on your device in private SQLite/Room databases and DataStore preferences. You are responsible for maintaining your device backups and configuration exports.
