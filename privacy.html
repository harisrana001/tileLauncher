Privacy-First Philosophy

TileLaunch ("we", "our", or "us") is designed with device autonomy at its core. We do not track, profile, log, or sell your personal data. All core launcher features—including installed apps navigation, notification counts, calendar schedules, and photo live tiles—process exclusively on your device. Free tier users experience a single non-intrusive interstitial advertisement upon completing first-run onboarding, while Pro users enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.

1. Permissions Requested & Local Processing

TileLaunch requests only the permissions strictly required to execute its duties as your primary device launcher:

1.1 Installed Applications (LauncherApps & Queries)

Purpose: As an Android home screen replacement, TileLaunch queries your device's installed applications to display them in the Start screen tile grid and the All Apps alphabetical drawer, and to launch them upon selection.

Data Handling: App names, icons, and package identifiers are inspected strictly in device memory. They are never transmitted, logged, or uploaded to any remote server.

1.2 Notification Access (NotificationListenerService)

Purpose: Used strictly to calculate numeric unread notification count badges on corresponding tiles (such as missed phone calls, unread messaging, or email totals).

Data Handling: TileLaunch inspects only package identifiers to aggregate count integers. TileLaunch never reads, inspects, stores, or transmits notification message text, sender identities, or notification contents.

1.3 Approximate Coarse Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Purpose: Requested optionally when you configure the Weather Live Tile. This allows the launcher to fetch temperature and forecast data from the open-source, keyless Open-Meteo API.

Data Handling: Approximate coordinates are sent directly to the Open-Meteo endpoint via encrypted HTTPS. Your location is never logged, stored in any external database, or shared with advertising partners. If permission is denied, weather functionality can be skipped or configured to fallback coordinates.

1.4 Calendar Events (READ_CALENDAR)

Purpose: Used optionally to display the next scheduled agenda appointment on the 3D flipping Calendar Live Tile.

Data Handling: Calendar queries operate locally via Android's built-in CalendarContract. Event titles and dates remain exclusively on your device and are never sent externally.

1.5 Photos & Wallpaper (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Purpose: Allows you to pick local photo wallpapers for your Start screen and Cover screen, and to cycle image slideshows on the Photos Live Tile.

Data Handling: Image files are accessed locally via the Android system photo picker or MediaStore. No images or metadata are ever uploaded to cloud servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

TileLaunch is committed to clean, transparent advertising practices without cluttering your daily smartphone workflow:

  • Single Placement: In the Free version of TileLaunch, exactly one interstitial advertisement may be shown upon completing the initial setup/onboarding flow.
  • Banned Placements: Advertisements are strictly prohibited on your Start screen, All Apps drawer, live tiles, settings screen, and Cover screen. You will never encounter banner ads or popups during normal phone use.
  • 100% Ad-Free Pro Tier: Users who upgrade to TileLaunch Pro enjoy a permanently ad-free experience. All ad requests and SDK initializations are completely disabled when Pro is active.
  • European Regulations (GDPR) & UMP: For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, TileLaunch integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. A consent dialog is presented prior to requesting ads, giving you complete autonomy over personalized vs. non-personalized advertising choices.
  • Ad Network Data Collection: Ad requests are serviced by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Google may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, and technical diagnostic telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. TileLaunch does not share any of your local app lists, notifications, photos, or calendar data with Google AdMob.

3. Google Play Billing (TileLaunch Pro)

TileLaunch offers an optional one-time in-app lifetime purchase ("TileLaunch Pro") to unlock all 20 accent colors, the Classic 2-column layout era pack, Live Tiles, and Cover screen.

Payment Processing: All transactions are executed securely through the official Google Play Billing library. TileLaunch never receives, handles, or stores your credit card details, banking credentials, or billing address.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All user preferences, layout grid arrangements, selected accent colors, and cached weather forecasts are stored locally in private SQLite (Room) databases and encrypted DataStore files on your phone.

Because TileLaunch does not operate user accounts or external cloud databases, uninstalling the TileLaunch application immediately and permanently erases all associated local data from your device.

6. Security

All network communications (including Open-Meteo weather lookups and Google Play purchase token validations) are strictly enforced over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
